    HOW TO PLAY

    1. Each day deals 8 clue cards. Five are true. Three are red herrings.
    2. Flip cards to read clues. Tap a flipped card to enlarge it.
    3. Flipping all 8 cards cuts that round's fish payout by 80%.
    4. Flipped cards can be burned — the bottom burns away first.
    5. Work out the 1–2 word answer and submit it. Correct = round over.
    6. Earn 12% of your final score in fish to spend in the shop.

    SCORING

      Your score never drops below zero — not even mid-round.
